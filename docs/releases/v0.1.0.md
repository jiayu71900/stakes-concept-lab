# BET I DO. v0.1 — The complete consequence loop

BET I DO. is now a playable public concept: make a promise about yourself,
put up a physical stake, let a stranger take the other side, and see what
happens when the promise fails.

## Play it

- Start with the three-screen preview: https://stakes-concept-lab.jiayu71900.chatgpt.site/preview
- Enter the full playable Demo: https://stakes-concept-lab.jiayu71900.chatgpt.site
- Join the product discussion: https://github.com/jiayu71900/stakes-concept-lab/discussions

## What v0.1 includes

- Five core surfaces: Discover, Bet Detail, Match, Outcome, and Profile.
- A complete Create → Discover → Challenge → Match → Fail → Ship or Default → Continue loop.
- Seven limited random pulls; ordinary bets cannot be searched.
- Variable 7, 14, 21, 30, or 60-day challenges with realistically spaced room updates.
- Visible defaults that do not ban people from publishing or challenging again.
- The Highest Stakes +10 consequence and the default-cleaning path.
- One persistent visitor name across publishing, challenging, and room messages.
- An opt-in visitor archive whose challenges and named messages can reappear in future random pulls.
- A three-page public preview that creates a named challenge ticket and adds it to the Demo.
- A Build With Us Lab and an end-of-experience contribution funnel.

## What this release is not

This is not a production betting, escrow, payment, identity, or fulfillment
system. The public repository contains the playable product surface, visible
rules, mock engines, documentation, and collaboration layer. Production trust,
verification, abuse prevention, payments, logistics, disputes, and commercial
operations remain outside this repository.

## How to contribute

Do not begin with a generic “I want to help.” Play the story, name one moment
or failure, and choose an open edge. The repository deliberately exposes
questions that can grow into durable product ownership.
