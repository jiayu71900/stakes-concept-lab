# Concept Demo Scenario

Target runtime: **60–90 seconds**.

1. **Create** — open the lightweight form; a draft passes through the state machine into the random pool.
2. **Discover** — meet an open bet through a limited random pull. Every refresh draws randomly rather than walking a fixed list; an owned visitor bet may appear as watch-only.
3. **Challenge** — inspect the physical stake and proof contract, then enter the draw.
4. **Match** — close entry and draw one challenger before the timer starts.
5. **Challenge Room** — start the variable-length clock, advance by one or seven days, reveal realistically spaced maker updates, and optionally leave one update for the current day.
6. **Outcome** — at the deadline, choose completed or missed. The main story follows missed into a 72-hour direct-shipping window.
7. **Default** — choose not to ship. The maker receives a visible mark but keeps all product access; only leaderboard eligibility is lost.
8. **Aftermath** — continue under the visitor’s own name as marked or unmarked. A marked visitor can challenge again; if that maker later defaults, the settlement visibly removes one unresolved mark.

Closing line:

> **Debt doesn’t ban you. It moves through the network.**

The primary Steam Deck path produces `1 → 0`. Entering a Highest Stakes bet demonstrates the separate `+10` liability.
