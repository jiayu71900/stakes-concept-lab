# Concept Demo Scenario

Target runtime: **60–90 seconds**.

1. **Create** — open the lightweight form; a draft passes through the state machine into the random pool.
2. **Discover** — meet an open pact through a limited random pull. Refresh to see that the pool is not searchable.
3. **Challenge** — inspect the physical stake and proof contract, then enter the draw.
4. **Match** — simulate selection. The challenger is chosen before the timer starts.
5. **Fail** — jump to the deadline. The maker misses the contract and enters a 72-hour direct-shipping window.
6. **Default** — choose not to ship. The maker receives a visible mark but keeps all product access; only leaderboard eligibility is lost.
7. **Cleansing** — weeks later, the maker is someone else’s challenger. That person defaults, cleansing one unresolved mark.

Closing line:

> **Debt doesn’t ban you. It moves through the network.**

The primary Steam Deck path produces `1 → 0`. Entering a Highest Stakes pact demonstrates the separate `+10` liability.
