# Open Problems

These questions are intentionally outside V0 and should not become accidental rules in UI code.

## Trust and abuse

- How do we stop collusive default-cleansing loops?
- Should cleansing compare stake values or default severity?
- How are fake ownership, empty boxes, damage, and tracking fraud handled?
- Who resolves proof disputes when the challenger has a direct incentive?

## Discovery

- How should controlled randomness balance novelty, fairness, and exposure budgets?
- What makes a pact “interesting” without turning it into rage bait?
- How should shared links coexist with ordinary no-search discovery?

## Operations

- Direct shipping versus physical escrow.
- Eligible item categories, valuation, insurance, prohibited goods, and cross-border limits.
- What evidence freezes a completion contract?

## Policy

- Random challenger selection around valuable physical items needs jurisdiction-specific legal review before a real launch.
- Product language does not determine legal classification.
- V0 uses mock interactions only; it does not accept money, items, or real entries.
